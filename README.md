# Retail Sales Analysis

A SQL & Excel-based analysis of retail sales performance using the Superstore dataset, exploring sales trends, profitability, and customer/product performance.

## Objective
Analyze retail sales data to uncover trends in revenue, profit, and order volume, and identify the top-performing products, regions, and customer segments.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Superstore dataset
- **Fields:** Order details, product category/sub-category, sales, profit, discount, region, customer segment, ship mode, order/ship dates

## Tools Used
- **SQL** — data cleaning, aggregation, and querying (joins, window functions, CTEs)
- **Excel** — pivot tables, charts, and dashboard visuals

## Analysis Performed
- Sales and profit trends over time (monthly/yearly)
- Performance by product category and sub-category
- Regional and state-level sales comparison
- Customer segment analysis (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- Discount impact on profit margins
- Top and bottom performing products

## Key Insights
- Technology generated the highest total profit (about $145.5K), followed by Office Supplies (about $122.5K), while Furniture lagged significantly behind (about $18.5K)
- Orders with discounts up to 20% remained profitable (peak avg. profit of about $96 at 10% discount), but discounts above 20% consistently resulted in losses, with the steepest average loss (about -$310) occurring at a 50% discount
- The West region led in total sales (about $725.5K), followed by East (about $678.8K) and Central (about $501.2K), while the South region had the lowest sales (about $391.7K)
